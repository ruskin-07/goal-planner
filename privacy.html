Privacy

Goal Planner is a simple calculator. Here's what happens to your information, in plain language.

What we collect

  • The numbers you enter. Your goal cost, savings, income, and expenses are used only to calculate your result, entirely in your browser — the manual form never sends this to a server. If you share a result link, those numbers are encoded in that link's web address, so anyone you send it to can see them.
  • Goal text, if you use the AI goal box. Typing your goal into the free-text "Tell us your goal" box sends that text to our server and to an AI provider, so it can be turned into the structured fields on the next screen. That text is not stored in a database — it only passes through minimal, short-retention server logs. If you use manual entry instead, none of this happens: your goal text and numbers stay fully in your browser.
  • Anonymous usage events. We use an analytics tool called PostHog to see which screens people view and which buttons they tap, so we can improve the tool. These events don't include your financial numbers.
  • Your email, only if you give it to us. If you choose to leave your email on the "monthly update" card, we store it with PostHog so we can identify you and, if we build that feature, send you an update. We don't use it for anything else.

What we don't do

No accounts or passwords. No ads. We don't sell your data. Nothing about your finances is saved to this device — close the tab and it's gone, unless you've saved or shared a result link yourself.
